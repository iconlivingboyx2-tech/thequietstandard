JOURNAL IV

Architecture Begins in Observation

Every lasting structure begins long before the first stone is placed.

Architecture begins with observation—understanding light, proportion, rhythm and the character of a place before attempting to shape it.

The same is true of ideas.

A philosophy built without observation becomes opinion. A life built without observation becomes habit.

To observe carefully is to resist the urge to act prematurely.

Only after attention comes structure.
